App Overview

简仓记 is a lightweight, streamlined local inventory management app designed for individuals, small shops, studios, and multi-warehouse businesses. No account registration or internet connection required — all data is securely stored on your local device. From item entry and stock-in/out operations to data analytics and low-stock alerts, 简仓记 covers the entire inventory management workflow.

Core Features

📦

Full Item Lifecycle Management

Record name, English name, model, barcode, location, quantity, price, supplier, images, and description — all in one place.

🏭

Multi-Warehouse Management

Create multiple independent warehouses with fully isolated data and switch between them in one tap.

🔄

Stock In / Out Records

Every operation is logged in detail with undo support; a timeline view traces the full stock history of any individual item.

📊

Data Analytics

Daily and monthly stock-in/out summaries; multi-dimensional analysis by category, supplier, and unit.

🔔

Stock Alerts

Set a minimum threshold for each item — get automatic reminders when stock runs low to prevent stockouts.

🏷️

Flexible Category System

Create custom two-level categories with color labels and browse all items by category.

🚚

Supplier Management

Maintain supplier names, contacts, and phone numbers, and link items to their source supplier.

📐

Unit Management

Define custom measurement units (pieces, boxes, kilograms, etc.) to suit different industry needs.

📷

Barcode Scanning

Scan barcodes or QR codes to quickly locate or add items — fast and effortless.

🔍

Full-Text Search

Quickly search by name, English name, model, or barcode with smart search suggestions.

📂

Batch Import & Export

Bulk-import item data via Excel; export inventory reports and images with sharing support.

🗑️

Recycle Bin & Data Protection

Deleted items go to the recycle bin and can be restored; supplier and category snapshots are preserved to prevent data loss.

Technology & Design

  • Fully Offline: All data is stored locally — no account needed, your privacy stays in your hands.
  • Fast & Responsive: Built with Flutter for a smooth UI and instant interactions.
  • Adaptive UI: Works perfectly on all screen sizes — iPhone and iPad alike.
  • Modern Design: Clean Material-style interface that's intuitive and easy to use.
  • Data Security: Local Hive database — fast operations, no data ever uploaded to the cloud.
